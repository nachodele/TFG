# Glosario de Expresiones en Texto Plano y No Plano

| **Expresión** | **Texto Plano**| **No Plano**            |
|---------------|----------------|-------------------------|
| dot           | ·              | \( \cdot \)             |
| cdot          | ⋅              | \( \cdot \)             |
| cup           | ∪              | \( \cup \)              |
| cap           | ∩              | \( \cap \)              |
| leq           | ≤              | \( \leq \)              |
| geq           | ≥              | \( \geq \)              |
| Sigma         | Σ              | \( \Sigma \)            |
| sigma         | σ              | \( \sigma \)            |
| Lambda        | Λ              | \( \Lambda \)           |
| lambda        | λ              | \( \lambda \)           |
| neq           | ≠              | \( \neq \)              |
| bigcup        | ⋃              | \( \bigcup \)           |
| infty         | ∞              | \( \infty \)            |
| epsilon       | ε              | \( \epsilon \)          |
| varepsilon    | ϵ              | \( \varepsilon \)       |
| notin         | ∉              | \( \notin \)            |
| in            | ∈              | \( \in \)               |
| setminus      | ∖              | \( \setminus \)         |
| overline      | -              | \( \overline{} \)       |
| forall        | ∀              | \( \forall \)           |
| subseteq      | ⊆              | \( \subseteq \)         |
| supseteq      | ⊇              | \( \supseteq \)         |
| to            | →              | \( \to \)               |
| sum           | ∑              | \( \sum \)              |
| product       | ∏              | \( \prod \)             |
| approx        | ≈              | \( \approx \)           |
| mid           | ∣              | \( {\mid} \)            |
| times         | x              | \( {\times} \)          |
| hat           | ^              | \( \hat{} \)            |
